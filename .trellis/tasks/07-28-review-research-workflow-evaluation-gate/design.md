# Design — F07 review gate

## Review independence

Primary authors of inventory/pilot cases cannot be sole final reviewers. At most one adjudication round for disagreements.

## Verdict document

`predecessor-gate-verdict.md` must include: verdict, named conditions (if conditional), blocking items (if blocked), frozen target sha256, evaluation_contract_version, reviewer IDs, date.

## Evaluation index

Lists all child research artifacts with paths and content hashes for audit trail.
