Read exact committed G132, G131, A11, accepted v1.3.0, A132-0, and Procedure evidence objects. Mutable package bytes, runtime behavior, harness results, and author scratch are never semantic authority.

## Mapping model

The lifecycle leaf carries the G132 mapping verbatim as a closed tagged union. Applicable rows require a codomain family; not-applicable rows require null. The author script enumerates the Cartesian product of 17 rows and 845 immutable lifecycle bindings, records the exact Boolean result, and reconciles 14,365/975/13,390.

## Candidate continuity

Report-v2, validator, and differential corrections remain governed by G131. Finding-004 changes only inside G131's existing direct regions and uses the G132 replacement. Mechanical propagation remains the exact G131 finite tables. Any unmatched pointer is a fifth change and fatal.

## Reproducibility

Strict parse, canonical compact output, one final LF, domain-separated digests, two clean generations, exact output manifest, and no cache files are mandatory.
